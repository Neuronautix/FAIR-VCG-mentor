# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions.
- Focus feedback on ideas and implementation, not individuals.
- Accept responsibility and apologize when mistakes happen.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, trolling, insults, or derogatory language.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Personal or political attacks, intimidation, or threats.
- Any conduct that is inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project or its community.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behavior, open a private security report through GitHub Security Advisories or contact the maintainers via repository administrators.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
